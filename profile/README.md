## Less *da capo*, so you can get to the *coda* sooner.

We build productivity tools that reduce repetitive work, lower stress, and give you more time to focus on what matters. By automating the tasks that keep sending you back to the beginning, we help you move through your work with fewer repeats and (hopefully) reach the finish line sooner, in a reproducible and shareable manner with whole teams.

We also design for **lower operating costs**. In [CI execution mode](https://phillarmonic.github.io/drun/getting-started/ai-integration/#saving-money-on-ai-usage-with-drun), **Drun** keeps routine output quiet and sends full logs to stdout or stderr only **when something fails**. This **reduces the amount of log data consumed by AI agents**, cuts unnecessary **input-token usage**, and makes automated workflows **cheaper to run at scale.**
